# Iron Sultanate Battlekit

Следующие предметы **Battlekit** уникальны для Warband'ов Iron Sultanate. Правила всех остальных предметов смотри в стандартном разделе Battlekit (▸ см. [[Digital Rulebook/Battlekit/Battlekit|Battlekit]] в *Trench Crusade Digital Rulebook*)

```dataview
TABLE WITHOUT ID
  file.link as "Iron Sultanate Battlekit",
  battlekit_type as "Battlekit Type",
  ducats as "👑",
  injury_modifier as "Injury Modifier",
  stipulations as "Stipulations",
  keywords as "Keywords"
FROM "Warbands of Trench Crusade/The Sultanate of the Iron Wall/Battlekit"
WHERE file.name != "Iron Sultanate Battlekit"
SORT file.name ASC
```
